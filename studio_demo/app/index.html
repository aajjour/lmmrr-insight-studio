SCIENCE & TRANSPARENCY

What the colors mean.

The interface separates reported calibrated evidence from purely descriptive pattern recognition.

●

RED · Calibrated signal

Holm-adjusted global-process p-value meets the selected threshold; requires supplied refit-bootstrap evidence and a complete declared family. Not a proven cause.

✓

GREEN · No signal detected

Eligible adjusted p-value exceeds the threshold. The null is not proved correct, and power may be insufficient.

◇

AMBER · Insufficient calibration

Visual excursions may be descriptive, but no eligible complete-family global inference is available.

D

DEMO · Illustrative only

Synthetic patterns can use red illustrations but always carry a demo label; they are not statistical tests or empirical results.
